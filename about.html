Our Story

Beyond spotless: cleaning as hospitality.

We founded Lustra because we were tired of the cleaning industry's race to the bottom. We chose to treat cleaning as a skill and a trade — paying living wages, selecting premium eco-chemistry, and honoring the architecture of your home.

Professional Vetting

The Lustra Professional Standard

1. Background Checks

Every professional undergoes a detailed identity check, local criminal history audit, and professional reference check before they step into your home.

2. Detailed Hospitality Training

Cleaners undergo a 20-hour practical program on premium home surfaces (marble, hardwood, brass) and guest-satisfaction etiquette standard in boutique hotels.

3. Living Wage Rates

We pay our cleaners significantly above standard market averages. Better compensation drives quality, retention, and pride in craft.

Eco-Commitments

Chemistry that respects the earth.

We reject industrial aerosols and chlorine bleach. Instead, we use biological ingredients that decompose cleanly back into organic elements.

Refillable Glass Refills

We use a zero-waste cartridge system. We carry concentrates and mix with local water in refillable aluminum containers, cutting shipping weight and carbon emissions by 90%.

Allergy & Pet Friendly

Our plant-based oils and natural detergents contain no harsh synthetics, ensuring child and pet surfaces are entirely chemical-free.

Carbon-Considered Routing

Our cleaning schedules are algorithmically clustered by neighborhood. This prevents long, multi-neighborhood drives and cuts down transport exhaust.

Common Enquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What cleaning products do you use? +

We clean with plant-derived, biodegradable formulas that are light on fragrance and free of synthetic dyes, phosphates, or chlorine. On request, we can use unscented formulations for highly sensitive individuals.

Do I need to be home for the cleaning? +

No. Most of our clients prefer to share lockbox codes, key entrance codes, or leave instructions for concierge desk pickups. All home keys are logged and secured in our system using end-to-end security protocols.

How does the 24-hour guarantee work? +

If any aspect of your cleaning does not meet your expectations, send us a photo or message within 24 hours of completion. We will schedule a follow-up visit to re-clean the area free of charge.

Can I pause or reschedule my subscription? +

Absolutely. Rescheduling or skipping a clean is completely free up to 24 hours before your slot. You can manage everything through your account or by dropping our support team an email.
