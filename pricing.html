What affects your price?

Lustra pricing models are calculated around average room layout dimensions. The following items can influence final calculations:

  • • **Additional bathrooms:** Bathrooms require high hygiene care and raise pricing margins.
  • • **Condition rescales:** Extremely neglected spaces may trigger a Deep Clean upgrade.
  • • **Pets:** We accommodate pets, but highly hairy areas may require extra detailing.
